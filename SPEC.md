## SPEC.md

### Vision / Goals & Non-goals
- **Goals:** Create a fun, engaging adventure-style platformer with classic levels and stages. Target audience includes children (8-12), teenagers (13-17), and adults (18+).
- **Non-goals:** Avoid overly complex puzzles or content that might alienate younger players.

### Initial tech stack
- Unity for game development
- C# as the primary scripting language
- Blender for 3D asset creation
- Tiled for level design

### First milestone
1. Design and implement classic levels and stages.
2. Integrate a variety of characters, including Mario, Luigi, and possibly new ones.
3. Add power-ups like mushrooms, stars, or fire flowers.
4. Ensure the game has an adventure-style narrative that aligns with the target audience's preferences.
